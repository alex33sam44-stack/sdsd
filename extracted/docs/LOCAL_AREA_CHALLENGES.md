# Local area challenges

This release adds local area pages that turn growth from a national feed into neighborhood-level challenges.

## Routes

- `/areas/faisal`
- `/areas/ramses`
- `/areas/nasr-city`

## What each area page shows

- Weekly local challenge headline.
- Most searched routes in the area.
- Current local reports.
- Top contributors and local badges.
- WhatsApp-first CTA to invite people from the same area.
- Community CTA to ask people on the same line.

## Growth purpose

The goal is to make sharing feel local and competitive:

- "تحدي خط فيصل هذا الأسبوع"
- "مين أكثر شخص ساعد ركاب رمسيس؟"
- "خلّي منطقتك تظهر على الخريطة"

This gives users a reason to invite friends from the same neighborhood instead of sharing a generic transit app.
