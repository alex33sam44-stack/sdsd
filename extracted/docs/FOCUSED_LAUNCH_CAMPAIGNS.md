# Focused launch campaigns

The product should not launch to all of Egypt at once. The first growth loop should target dense, painful, daily commuting segments where people already coordinate inside WhatsApp groups.

## Added campaign pages

- `/cairo-university` — students going to lectures/sections from Faisal, Ramses, Nasr City, etc.
- `/ramses` — heavy interchange commuters and employees changing routes through Ramses.
- `/faisal` — Faisal/Haram commuters with crowded daily routes.
- `/nasr-city` — Nasr City workers/students dealing with peak-hour congestion.

## Each landing page includes

- Audience-specific promise and pain point.
- Immediate demo result: time, fare, transfers, traffic.
- Hot routes for that segment.
- WhatsApp group CTA.
- Link to `/planner` with prefilled `from`, `to`, and `src=launch-*`.
- Link to the local area community/challenge where applicable.

## Launch goal

Start with one or two high-pain clusters, measure usage and sharing, then expand. Suggested first cluster:

1. Cairo University students
2. Faisal/Haram commuters
3. Ramses interchange commuters

Track with the existing growth events and `src=launch-*` query parameters.
